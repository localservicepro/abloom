An Arborist, Not Just a Bloke With a Chainsaw

Professional tree services built on real arboriculture

Abloom Tree Care is a local tree services business owned and operated by qualified arborist Jake DeFranceschi, based at 849 Tallagandra Lane in Sutton — twenty minutes from the centre of Canberra. We look after trees the way an arborist should: assess first, then prune, remove, brace or treat based on what the tree and the site actually need.

That means correct pruning cuts that keep trees healthy, rigging and lowering techniques that protect your home and gardens, and honest advice when a tree is worth saving rather than removing. It also means the paperwork side is covered — tree management plans, health assessments and VALID tree risk assessments that councils and insurers accept.

Common Questions

What people ask us first

How much does tree removal cost in Canberra?

It depends on the tree's size, species, condition and how close it stands to houses, power lines and other structures. A small tree in open lawn costs far less than a large gum that has to be dismantled in sections over a roof. We provide free, fixed quotes across Canberra and the surrounding districts, so you know the exact price before any work starts.

Do I need council approval to remove a tree in the ACT?

Often, yes. Many trees in the ACT are protected, and removing or significantly pruning a regulated or registered tree usually requires approval from the ACT Government. Rules differ again for NSW properties around Queanbeyan, Yass and Bungendore. We can assess your tree, tell you whether approval is required and prepare the supporting arborist report for your application.

Do you service Queanbeyan, Yass and Bungendore as well as Canberra?

Yes. We're based in Sutton, just north of Canberra, and service the whole region: Canberra and the ACT, Queanbeyan, Bungendore, Yass, Bywong, Wamboin, Sutton, Gundaroo, Gunning and Murrumbateman. Free quotes are available across all of these areas.

Can you provide an arborist report for a DA application in Canberra?

Yes. We prepare tree management plans and arborist reports to support development applications, along with health condition assessments, VALID tree risk assessments and root assessments. Reports are written to satisfy councils, planners, insurers and certifiers in the ACT and surrounding NSW council areas.

Do you offer emergency tree removal after storms in Canberra?

Yes. When storms bring down limbs or leave a tree hanging over your home, we provide rapid emergency tree removal across Canberra, the ACT and the surrounding NSW districts. We'll make the site safe, remove the hazard and help with documentation for your insurer.

Are you fully insured and qualified arborists?

Yes. Abloom Tree Care is run by qualified arborists and carries full public liability insurance. Every job — from routine hedge trimming to complex removals near structures — is planned and carried out to Australian arboricultural standards, and we're happy to show proof of qualifications and insurance with your quote.

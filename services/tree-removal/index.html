Tree lopping in Canberra, done the arborist’s way

People search for tree lopping in Canberra when they want a tree cut down or cut back — and it’s worth knowing that “lopping” means something specific to an arborist. True lopping (indiscriminately cutting branches back to stubs) is poor practice: it stresses the tree, invites decay and produces weakly attached regrowth that becomes tomorrow’s hazard. At Abloom Tree Care we deliver what most people actually want when they search for lopping: professional tree removal or correct reduction, carried out by qualified arborists who leave your property safe and your remaining trees healthy.

Whether it’s a dead gum leaning over the driveway in Watson, a storm-split eucalypt in Queanbeyan or a row of pines shading a Yass paddock, we remove trees of every size across Canberra, the ACT and the surrounding NSW districts.

How we remove trees safely

Very few Canberra trees stand in an open paddock. Most grow beside houses, fences, pools, power lines and garden beds — which is why our removals are planned, not improvised:

  • Assessment first. We check the tree’s condition, lean, targets and access, and confirm whether ACT or NSW approval rules apply before anything is cut.
  • Sectional dismantling. On tight sites, the tree comes down piece by piece — climbed, rigged and lowered under control rather than felled.
  • Modern rigging and lowering gear that protects roofs, fences and gardens beneath the canopy.
  • Full clean-up. Branches chipped, timber cut for firewood or removed, and the site raked before we leave. Add stump grinding to finish the job completely.

Do you need approval to remove a tree in Canberra?

Quite possibly. Many established trees in the ACT are protected, and removing or heavily pruning them without approval can attract significant penalties. NSW properties around Queanbeyan, Bungendore and Yass have their own rules. We’ll tell you up front whether your tree is likely to need approval, and we can prepare the supporting arborist report if it does — one crew for the paperwork and the chainsaw work.

Common Questions

Tree removal questions, answered

What's the difference between tree lopping and tree removal?

Tree removal takes the whole tree down to a stump using planned cuts and rigging. Lopping, in the traditional sense, hacks branches back to stubs — poor practice that stresses the tree and creates hazardous regrowth. If you searched for “tree lopping Canberra”, what you almost certainly want is professional removal or correct pruning, and we’ll recommend whichever genuinely suits your tree.

Can you remove trees close to houses or power lines?

Yes. Tight-access, high-consequence removals are our specialty. We dismantle trees in sections, rigging and lowering each piece under control so nothing touches your roof, fence or garden. Work near live power lines is coordinated with the network operator where required.

How much does it cost to remove a tree in Canberra?

Price depends on the tree’s size, condition and what surrounds it. A small open-lawn tree is a quick job; a large gum over a house takes a full crew, rigging and most of a day. Every job gets a free, fixed written quote before we start — call 0423 838 689.

What happens to the tree after removal?

Whatever suits you: branches are chipped into mulch you can keep for the garden, trunk wood can be cut to firewood lengths, or we take the lot away. We can also grind the stump below ground level so you can replant, pave or lay turf.

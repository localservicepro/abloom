Emergency tree removal in Canberra, when it matters most

Canberra’s storms don’t make appointments. When wind, hail or saturated ground brings a tree down — or worse, leaves it half-fallen and hanging — you need emergency tree removal in Canberra from a crew that can make the scene safe without making it worse. Abloom Tree Care responds fast across the ACT and the surrounding NSW districts, with the climbing skills, rigging gear and insurance to deal with trees in the worst possible positions.

What counts as a tree emergency?

  • A tree or large limb fallen on a house, shed, car or fence
  • A split or cracked trunk, or a limb hanging half-detached over an area people use
  • A tree leaning suddenly after storm or root disturbance, or lifting soil at its base
  • Branches tangled in power lines — stay well clear and report it; we work in with the network operator
  • A blocked driveway or access road on a rural block after a storm

If a tree is threatening people right now, keep everyone away from the area and call us on 0423 838 689. Describe what you can see and we’ll tell you honestly whether it needs us tonight or can safely wait for daylight — storm-damaged trees are unpredictable, and this is no place for ladder-and-chainsaw improvisation.

How we handle emergency callouts

First, make it safe: assess the failure, secure or remove the immediate hazard, and get weight off damaged structures using controlled rigging — not gravity. Then, clear up: debris chipped and removed, the site left usable again. Finally, the follow-through most crews skip: we photograph the damage for your insurance claim, and we can inspect the rest of your trees for the next failure waiting to happen. If a damaged tree can be saved with pruning or bracing rather than removal, we’ll tell you — see our tree pruning service.

Common Questions

Emergency tree work, answered

A tree has fallen on my house — what should I do first?

Get everyone out from under the affected rooms, stay clear of the tree (fallen trees shift), and don’t touch anything near wires. Then call us on 0423 838 689. We’ll make the structure safe with controlled rigging and help you document the damage for your insurer.

Does insurance cover emergency tree removal?

Home policies commonly cover removal of a tree that has damaged an insured structure, though policies differ on debris removal and preventive work. We photograph everything, describe the failure and provide the paperwork insurers ask for — it’s part of the service.

How fast can you get to us?

From Sutton we cover Canberra and the surrounding districts quickly — for genuine make-safe emergencies we prioritise immediately, day or night. During major regional storm events we triage the most dangerous situations first, and we’ll always give you an honest arrival window when you call.

The tree is near power lines — can you still help?

Yes, but safety rules apply: never approach a tree in contact with wires. Report line contact to the electricity network first; once the lines are made safe or isolated, we complete the tree work. We can help coordinate that process during your callout.

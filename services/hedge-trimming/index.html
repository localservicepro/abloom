Hedge pruning in Canberra for homes and commercial grounds

Great hedge pruning in Canberra is equal parts horticulture and straight lines. Prune at the wrong time or cut past the green growth on the wrong species, and a hedge can take years to recover, or never fill back in at all. Our team prunes for looks and for plant health: crisp faces and level tops, taken at the right depth and the right time of year for photinia, pittosporum, viburnum, conifers, lilly pilly, box and every other hedge the capital region grows.

What we do for hedges

  • Formal shaping – dead-flat tops and faces on feature hedges, from knee-high box borders to two-storey privacy screens.
  • Height and width reduction – bringing overgrown hedges back to a maintainable size in staged, species-appropriate cuts.
  • Restoration work – renovating neglected or gappy hedges and advising honestly when replacement is the better investment.
  • Scheduled maintenance – set-and-forget visits that keep hedges permanently sharp for homes, strata and commercial grounds.
  • Full clean-up – every clipping raked, blown and removed. The only evidence we were there is the hedge.

Tall hedges are our home ground

Because we’re arborists, hedge height doesn’t worry us. Screens and windbreaks that have grown past ladder height, such as the leyland cypress rows around Wamboin and Murrumbateman, or mature photinia screens in the older suburbs, are pruned safely with the same climbing and access skills we use for tree pruning. If your hedge has quietly become a row of trees, we’re the right people to call.

Most hedge visits pair naturally with other work: shaping smaller ornamental trees, clearing regrowth off fence lines, or grinding out the stump of a hedge plant that’s died (see stump grinding). Common Questions

Hedge pruning questions, answered

How often should hedges be pruned in Canberra?

Most formal hedges look their best with two to three prunes a year, typically late spring and late summer, avoiding cuts right before heavy frost. Fast growers like photinia may want more, while most conifers need careful, lighter work. We’ll recommend a schedule for your species when we visit.

Can you reduce the height of an overgrown hedge?

Usually, yes, but how far and how fast depends on the species. Many hedge plants regenerate well from harder cuts, while most conifers won’t reshoot from bare wood, so reductions are staged to keep the hedge green. We’ll give you an honest assessment before cutting.

Do you take the clippings away?

Yes. Every job includes a full clean-up, with clippings raked and blown off paths, lawns and beds, then chipped or removed. You can also keep the chip as garden mulch if you’d like.

Do you service commercial and strata properties?

Yes. We maintain hedges and grounds trees for commercial sites, strata complexes and rental properties across Canberra and Queanbeyan, with scheduled visits, tidy sites and full insurance documentation available for property managers.

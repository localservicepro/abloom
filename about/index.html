Our Story

Tree care with the "care" put back in

Abloom Tree Care is owned and operated by Jake DeFranceschi, a qualified arborist based at 849 Tallagandra Lane in Sutton — a village twenty minutes north of Canberra's city centre. From there, our crew works across the whole capital region every week: the leafy inner suburbs, the newer districts, and the rural blocks of Queanbeyan, Yass, Bungendore, Wamboin, Bywong, Gundaroo, Gunning and Murrumbateman.

The name is the philosophy. Plenty of operators will cut anything for a price; we practise arboriculture. That means assessing before cutting, pruning to Australian standards so trees stay healthy, using rigging that protects your property, and telling you honestly when a tree should be saved — or when it genuinely has to go.

It's also why our work doesn't stop at the chainsaw. Abloom's consultancy arm prepares tree management plans for DA approvals, VALID tree risk assessments, health and root assessments, and treatment programs for pests like elm leaf beetle — the paperwork side of tree care that most crews can't offer.

Our Story

Tree management built on best arboriculture practices

Abloom Tree Care is a Canberra arboriculture company, run by qualified arborists. Working within Canberra, our crew covers the whole capital region every week: the inner suburbs, the ACT's outer districts, and the rural properties of Queanbeyan, Yass, Bungendore, Wamboin, Bywong, Gundaroo, Gunning and Murrumbateman.

The name is the philosophy. Plenty of tree cutters will cut anything for a price; we practise arboriculture. That means assessing before cutting, pruning to AS 4373-2007 so trees stay healthy, using rigging that protects your property, and telling you honestly when a tree should be saved, or when it genuinely has to go.

It's also why our work doesn't stop at the chainsaw. With an AQF 5 arborist, Abloom's consultancy arm prepares Tree Management Plans (TMP) for DA submission, VALID tree risk assessments, tree health and root assessments, and pest treatment programs. The project arborist is engaged to see your development works from start to finish.

What We Stand For

Four things every client can count on

Qualified, insured, accountable

AQF level 5 (consulting) and AQF level 3 (climbing) arborists, plus full public liability insurance on every job. We're happy to show proof of both with your quote.

The tree's health comes first

Correct pruning cuts, honest retain-or-remove advice, habitat checks on hollow-bearing trees, and treatment options before removal is ever recommended.

Your property, protected

Controlled rigging and lowering over roofs, pools and gardens, disciplined site safety, and a clean-up that leaves the place tidier than we found it. Houses, fences and gardens are protected before a single cut is made.

Straight answers

Fixed written quotes, realistic timeframes, and advice on ACT and NSW tree rules before work starts. No surprises at any stage.
